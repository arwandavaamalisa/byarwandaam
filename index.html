About

Detail-oriented by habit, not by title.

I'm a Personal Assistant and Technical Virtual Assistant with over three years supporting executives, clinics, and growing e-commerce brands. My work sits at the intersection of three things: keeping a calendar honest, keeping a customer happy, and keeping a system running without babysitting it.

Day to day that looks like managing inboxes and appointments, answering customers across WhatsApp and email, and building the CRM workflows and website automations that quietly remove repetitive work. I've supported a neurology clinic's patient scheduling, a Canadian agency's GoHighLevel automations, and a Dutch skincare brand's booking system — each one asking for the same core skill: notice what's slowing things down, then fix it.
